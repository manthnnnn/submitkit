About the Filtering Algorithm

This system uses a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier trained on thousands of labeled spam and ham (legitimate) emails. It applies Bayes' Theorem with strong (naive) independence assumptions between the features (words).

By converting the email text into a TF-IDF matrix, the model can weigh the importance of certain vocabulary (like "urgent", "lottery", or "verify") and calculate the mathematical probability that an email belongs to the SPAM class.
