How VADER Works

VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) uses a hand-crafted lexicon of words with sentiment scores, combining them with grammatical rules to produce compound polarity scores.

▲ compound ≥ 0.05 → POSITIVE
▼ compound ≤ −0.05 → NEGATIVE
● between → NEUTRAL

About VADER NLP Model

VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) is a rule-based sentiment analysis tool specifically designed for social media text. It was developed by C.J. Hutto and Eric Gilbert at Georgia Tech.

Unlike ML-based sentiment models that require training data, VADER uses a lexicon of over 7,500 features with known polarity combined with grammatical rules for capitalization, punctuation, and degree modifiers.
