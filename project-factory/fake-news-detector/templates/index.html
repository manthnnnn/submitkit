About the NLP Architecture

This detector uses a Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) vectorizer to extract features from news articles, followed by a Passive-Aggressive Classifier to categorize the text as REAL or FAKE.

Passive-Aggressive algorithms are online learning algorithms. They remain passive for correct classifications, and turn aggressive when a miscalculation occurs, updating their internal states. This makes them highly effective for massive NLP streams.
