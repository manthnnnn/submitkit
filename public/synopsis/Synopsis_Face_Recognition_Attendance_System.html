1. Abstract

Traditional attendance tracking in educational institutions and enterprise environments heavily relies on paper-based sign-in sheets or fingerprint biometric hardware. These conventional modalities suffer from significant limitations, including physical proxy marking, identity theft, operational latency during peak arrival intervals, and sanitary vulnerabilities. This project introduces a contactless, automated biometric attendance architecture utilizing deep convolutional feature extraction and real-time facial recognition. By embedding an active eye-blink and micro-texture anti-spoofing mechanism, the proposed platform successfully eliminates 2D photographic and video-replay presentation attacks. The system integrates automated daily attendance logging into relational databases, an analytical administrative dashboard, and automated email/SMS deficit notifications for parents and mentors.

2. Problem Statement

Manual attendance registers consume 10–15% of instructional lecture time and are susceptible to deliberate proxy attendances. Hardware biometric terminals (e.g., optical fingerprint scanners) experience severe queuing bottlenecks and fail under uncooperative dermatological conditions. Furthermore, rudimentary webcam facial recognition scripts are routinely deceived by high-resolution smartphone photographs held before the optical sensor. There exists a critical necessity for a non-intrusive, real-time facial recognition system equipped with hardware-independent liveness verification, automated timestamping, and faculty audit reporting.

3. Objectives of the Proposed Work

  • Sub-Second Multi-Face Detection: Detect and track human frontal faces within live RTSP/USB video feeds under fluctuating ambient illumination.
  • 128-Dimensional Facial Encoding: Extract discriminative Euclidean embeddings using a pre-trained Deep Residual Network (ResNet-34) to achieve a recognition accuracy exceeding 98.4%.
  • Presentation Attack Defense (Anti-Spoofing): Implement Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) blink calculation and chromatic distortion heuristics to ensure authentic physical subject presence.
  • Automated Database Logging: Timestamp real-time entry and exit logs without manual supervisory intervention.
  • Faculty Administration Portal: Provide an intuitive graphical interface for batch student enrollment, timetable scheduling, and 1-click Excel/PDF report generation.

5. Modular Breakdown

  1. Acquisition & Preprocessing Module: Captures video frames at 30 FPS, converts BGR streams into normalized grayscale and RGB channels, and normalizes histogram variations.
  2. Facial Landmark Localization: Identifies 68 facial fiducial coordinates utilizing dlib's Kazemi-Sullivan shape predictor.
  3. Active Liveness Detection Module: Computes the vertical-to-horizontal distance ratio of eye landmarks over sequential temporal windows to detect involuntary saccadic blinks.
  4. Biometric Matching Engine: Computes Euclidean distance matrix: D(x, y) = || f(x) - f(y) ||_2 against enrolled student profiles; matches with threshold < 0.6 are marked present.
  5. Database & Analytics Module: Logs Student ID, Name, Department, Date, Timestamp, and Confidence Level into SQLite/PostgreSQL with instant CSV/Excel export.
