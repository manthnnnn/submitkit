### 1. Abstract
The rapid proliferation of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion synthesis architectures has facilitated the creation of hyper-realistic digital face-swaps, audio impersonations, and synthetic video manipulations commonly known as "deepfakes." These synthetic media pose severe societal threats, including corporate CEO fraud, political disinformation, identity theft, and defamatory extortion. This project presents *Deepfake Sentinel*, an end-to-end multimedia forensics platform capable of classifying uploaded video files and live video streams as authentic or computationally synthesized. By combining spatial convolutional feature extractors (EfficientNet / Meso-4) with recurrent temporal sequence networks (LSTM), the architecture analyzes frame-to-frame boundary inconsistencies, abnormal ocular blink dynamics, and frequency-domain artifacts invisible to human observation.

---

### 2. Problem Statement
Human visual inspection fails to reliably discern state-of-the-art synthetic media generated by advanced autoencoders and latent diffusion models. Traditional image forensic techniques that inspect EXIF metadata or discrete cosine transform (DCT) coefficients fall short because social media platforms routinely re-encode and compress video uploads, destroying high-frequency forensic residues. There is an urgent requirement for an automated, deep-learning-driven forensic verification architecture capable of evaluating both static intra-frame warping boundaries and inter-frame temporal continuity to deliver verifiable probability scores and forensic interpretability heatmaps.

---

### 3. Objectives of the Proposed System
1. **Automated Facial Extraction & Alignment:** Decompose incoming MP4/AVI videos into discrete temporal frames, isolate facial bounding regions, and align landmarks at 30 FPS.
2. **Spatial Artifact Identification:** Detect subtle blending boundaries, unnatural skin smoothing, and illumination mismatches around facial borders via deep convolutional filters.
3. **Temporal Inconsistency Tracking:** Utilize Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) networks to track inter-frame unnatural jitter, inconsistent blinking patterns, and facial landmark trajectory anomalies.
4. **Explainable AI (XAI) Forensic Heatmaps:** Integrate Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) to highlight the precise pixel regions influencing the fake classification decision.
5. **Interactive Web Inspection Portal:** Deliver a clean, modern user dashboard allowing drag-and-drop video uploads with real-time progress bars, confidence score meters, and downloadable forensic PDF audit reports.

### 5. Modular Breakdown
- **Module 1: Media Ingestion & Decomposition:** Decodes video files, standardizes frame rates (24–30 FPS), and normalizes resolution dimensions.
- **Module 2: ROI Face Crop Pipeline:** Isolates the facial bounding polygon, applies affine transformations to align eye centers horizontally, and scales to 224x224 RGB tensors.
- **Module 3: Spatial Feature Extraction (CNN):** Passes individual frames through an EfficientNet-B4 / MesoInception neural network to extract 1024-dimensional feature representations of compression and blending artifacts.
- **Module 4: Temporal Analysis (Recurrent Layer):** Feeds consecutive feature vectors across 20–40 frame sequences into a Bi-LSTM network to detect temporal flickering and unnatural motion vectors.
- **Module 5: Forensic Decision & Visualization:** Generates a binary authenticity probability score (0.0 to 1.0) along with an overlay Grad-CAM heatmap highlighting falsified biometric features.
